Ryan OpenClaw – Privacy policy

Effective 23 September 2026.

Ryan OpenClaw is a private personal assistant that Ryan runs for his own use. Only Ryan's own Google accounts are connected to it. It does not offer accounts or services to anyone else.

Google data it accesses

With Ryan's consent on Google's sign-in screen, the assistant can access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts data in Ryan's accounts. It uses that data only to carry out tasks Ryan asks for or has authorised, including recurring or proactive tasks he has set up, such as scheduled checks and reminders. Examples include finding or summarising email, drafting or sending a reply, adding a calendar event, or updating a document or spreadsheet.

How it is stored

  • Google sign-in tokens (OAuth refresh tokens) are kept in an encrypted keyring on the computer that runs the assistant.
  • Google content is fetched when a task needs it. Content used in a task may be kept in the assistant's conversation history, logs and notes on that computer.

Who else processes it

  • AI model providers. To understand a request and produce an answer, the assistant sends the request and the Google content relevant to it to the AI model providers Ryan has configured, currently OpenAI and Anthropic. They handle that content under their own terms, their privacy policies (OpenAI, Anthropic) and Ryan's account settings with them. This policy does not describe or guarantee how long they keep content or whether they use it to improve their services.
  • Chat apps. Messages from the assistant, which can quote or summarise Google data, reach Ryan through OpenClaw WebChat (the assistant's own chat interface) and the messaging channels he has connected to it, such as Discord. Messages sent through a third-party channel are handled under that service's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Recipients Ryan chooses. For example, people Ryan asks or authorises the assistant to email.

What it does not do

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except as described above. Ryan does not use it to train AI models.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account linked apps page. Stored tokens can also be deleted from the assistant's computer with gog auth remove.
